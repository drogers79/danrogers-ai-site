About Dan

I Help Local Businesses Find What AI Can Actually Do for Them.

I'm Dan Rogers, an AI consultant based in Williamsburg, VA. I work directly with business owners and operators to cut through the noise around AI — figuring out what's genuinely useful for their specific situation and building it.

Most of what's written about AI is either too abstract or aimed at enterprise companies. I focus on the businesses that make up the backbone of our local economy — the ones where a few well-placed AI tools can actually change how the owner spends their week.

👤

Why AI Consulting for Local Businesses?

The businesses I see around Williamsburg and across Virginia are run by people who are great at what they do but stretched thin doing it. They're spending hours on things that, with the right setup, could happen automatically.

The opportunity is real, but most of the information out there assumes you have a dedicated tech team or a big budget. I started this practice because I wanted to change that — to bring the same productivity gains that large companies are seeing to a 10-person operation in Hampton Roads.

I'm not selling software. I'm not pushing a platform. I look at how your business actually runs and tell you honestly where AI can help, how much it would cost, and whether it's worth doing right now.

How I Work

Every engagement starts with an honest look at your business before anything gets built. That's the AI Opportunity Assessment — a 90-minute deep dive that ends with a written map of your best opportunities, ranked by impact and effort.

From there, I help implement the things that make sense. Some clients take the report and run with it themselves. Others want me involved in building and deploying the tools. Both are fine.
